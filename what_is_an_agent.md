AI models and agents are all the rage. This article tries to provide a mental model of how agents work and the various concepts.

There are a lot of suble details and implementation differences in real world agents. I do not intent to present a fully factional representation, but rather provide a rough mental model that helps understand how agents operate. The goal is to help you become better at working with agents, understanding how to get the best results. I find having a good general mental model of how things work often subltly help your day-to-day use.
